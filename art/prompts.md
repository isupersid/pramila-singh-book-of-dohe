## Base prompt

> You are creating a cohesive series of illustrations for "मन के मोती," a family memory book containing Hindi dohe written by Pramila Singh.
>
> Maintain this exact visual identity across every illustration:
>
> - Hand-painted Indian watercolor and gouache on warm, lightly textured handmade paper
> - Graceful traditional Indian visual language with a contemporary editorial-art sensibility
> - Warm burgundy, muted saffron, antique gold, earthy green, soft indigo and natural cream palette
> - Gentle, dignified, reflective and emotionally warm
> - Symbolic visual storytelling rather than overly literal scenes
> - Delicate brushwork, subtle paper grain, restrained details and soft natural lighting
> - Portrait orientation, using Gemini's native 3:4 aspect ratio
> - High resolution, preferably at least 1600 x 2000 pixels when supported
> - Keep every essential subject fully inside an 8% safe margin on all sides; decorative paper texture and nonessential washes may extend to the edges
> - Preserve the downloaded image's original dimensions and bytes; do not crop, stretch, resize-to-fill or otherwise alter the generated composition
> - Place the strongest visual elements around the edges and lower portions while keeping some calm, lower-detail space through the center for possible text overlay on phones
> - No written words, letters, captions, calligraphy, signatures, numbers, logos or decorative typography
> - Do not imitate any named artist
> - Avoid glossy 3D rendering, photographic realism, cartoon styling and oversaturated colors
> - Religious figures and symbols must be portrayed respectfully
> - People should have natural anatomy, hands and expressions
> - The completed image should feel like one page from a treasured, hand-illustrated Indian poetry book
>
> For each request, first understand the meaning and emotional contrast of the supplied doha, then express that meaning as a single unified scene.
>
> Generate exactly one finished illustration without adding any text.
